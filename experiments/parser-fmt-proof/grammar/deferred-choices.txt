# The choices of the grammar that the Lean model of root ADR 0351 does not
# prove yet (docs/roadmap.md). check-deferred.mjs fails when this list and
# the unsupported choices differ: remove a line when its choice is proved.
File choice 1: BlankLines? (&. Blocks(0) BlankLines?)?
Blocks choice 1: Block(ind) (BlankLines? &(Ind(ind) !BlankLine .) Block(ind))*
BlockGroup choice 1: cs:BlockCommand(ind)+ (&{ not (or (take 1 (reverse cs))) } GroupBody(ind))?
GroupBody choice 1: Div(ind)
GroupBody choice 2: &(Ind(ind) !BlockCommandStart ![ \t] !BlankLine) AttributedBlock(ind)
Div choice 1: Ind(ind) DivMark Sp (Nl / !.) DivBlocks(fenced ind) (BlankLines? Ind(ind) DivMark Sp (Nl / !.))?
DivBlocks choice 1: (BlankLines? !(Ind(ind) DivLine) &(Ind(ind) ![ \t] !BlankLine .) Block(ind))*
BlockCommand choice 1: Ind(ind) At SetIdent Ws SetName Sp Eq ExprBody (Nl / !.)
BlockCommand choice 2: Ind(ind) At Ident Arguments Sp (Nl / !.) b:CommandBody(ind)?
CommandBody choice 1: &(Ind(col ind + 2) ![ \t] !BlankLine .) Blocks(col ind + 2)
BulletList choice 1: ListItem(ind, 0) (BlankLines? ListItem(ind, 0))*
OrderedList choice 1: ListItem(ind, 1) (BlankLines? ListItem(ind, 1))*
ListItem choice 1: Ind(ind) ListMark(ordered) Ws Inline(col ind + 2)+ (Nl / !.) ItemTail(col ind + 2)
ItemTail choice 1: (BlankLines? &(Ind(ind) ![ \t] !BlankLine .) Block(ind))*
DefList choice 1: DefGroup(ind) (BlankLines? &(Ind(ind) '=' [ \t]) DefGroup(ind))*
DefGroup choice 1: DefTerm(ind)+ DefBody(ind) (BlankLines? DefNew(ind) DefBody(ind))*
DefBody choice 1: &(Ind(col ind + 2) ![ \t] !BlankLine .) Block(col ind + 2) (BlankLines? &(Ind(col ind + 2) ![ \t] !BlankLine .) Block(col ind + 2))*
Table choice 1: h:TableRow(ind) Nl (TableDelimiter(ind))^table_delimiter TableBodyRow(ind, h)* (!(Nl Ind(ind) Pipe))^table_row (Nl / !.)
TableBodyRow choice 1: Nl r:TableRow(ind) (&{ r == h })^table_cells
TableRow choice 1: Ind(ind) cs:TableCellAndPipe+ Pipe Sp RowBreak?
SkippedReserved choice 1: '[!' ReservedBracketBody ']'
ReservedBracketBody choice 1: ('\\' ![\n] . / '%%' (!'%%' ![\n] .)* '%%' / '[' ReservedBracketBody ']' / ![\[\]\\\n] .)*
ReservedBracketBody choice 2: '%%' (!'%%' ![\n] .)* '%%'
ReservedBracketBody choice 3: '[' ReservedBracketBody ']'
